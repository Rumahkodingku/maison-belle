# Maison Belle — Design Direction

**Version:** 1.1  
**Status:** Approved  
**Brand direction:** Rose Pink · Balanced Luxury  
**Visual concept:** Editorial Beauty Atelier

---

## 1. Creative Direction

Maison Belle adopts **Rose Pink Editorial Luxury**: a premium, feminine beauty-studio identity built around a confident rose-pink brand color, balanced by warm ivory, soft blush, and deep rose-brown typography.

The design should feel:

- Feminine, without feeling childish.
- Premium, without feeling cold or inaccessible.
- Editorial, without sacrificing usability.
- Warm, welcoming, and confident.
- Photography-led, with deliberate whitespace and typography.

The rose-pink identity must be recognizable, but the entire website should not become a solid pink canvas. Warm ivory and soft blush preserve visual breathing room and editorial sophistication.

## 2. Brand Color Character

**Primary pink:** Rose Pink `#C97991`

Supporting colors:

- Dusty Rose: `#DDA6B5`
- Soft Blush: `#F3DDE3`
- Warm Ivory: `#FFF9F6`
- Deep Rose Brown: `#593D45`
- Champagne Beige: `#E9D7C8`

### Balanced Luxury contrast

The selected contrast direction is **Balanced Luxury**:

- Rose Pink is used for brand recognition, primary actions, active states, and selected details.
- Warm Ivory remains the primary page canvas.
- Soft Blush supports selected section backgrounds and subtle panels.
- Deep Rose Brown carries headings and body text for readability.
- Dusty Rose and Champagne Beige provide restrained decorative accents.

Do not use Rose Pink for long body text on light backgrounds unless contrast has been tested and meets accessibility requirements. Prefer Deep Rose Brown for text.

## 3. Suggested Color Distribution

Approximate visual balance:

- 40% Warm Ivory
- 25% Soft Blush
- 15% Rose Pink
- 10% Dusty Rose
- 10% Deep Rose Brown

This is a creative guideline, not a rigid measurement. Real pages may vary by section and imagery.

## 4. Typography

- Display and editorial headings: Playfair Display, regular or medium.
- Body and interface: DM Sans.
- Navigation and buttons: DM Sans medium.
- Use serif typography for expressive headlines and sans-serif typography for functional information.

Keep headings spacious and editorial. Keep forms, service details, prices, and booking instructions highly legible.

## 5. Layout and Imagery

- Use editorial asymmetry and generous whitespace.
- Use large, carefully cropped beauty photography.
- Mix portrait, landscape, and close-up imagery.
- Keep service information and booking actions easy to scan.
- Use minimal borders and restrained shadows.
- Avoid generic pink gradients, excessive rounded cards, and decorative clutter.
- Ensure mobile layouts are art-directed rather than merely compressed desktop layouts.

| Services            | Soft blush or ivory surfaces, editorial image-led composition                      |

| About               | Warm editorial split layout, story-led typography                                  |

## 7. Interaction Principles

- Primary CTA uses Rose Pink with a sufficiently contrasting foreground.
- Selected service, date, time, and gallery filter use clear selected states.
- Focus indicators must be visible and not rely on color alone.
- Hover and entrance animations should be subtle.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The booking flow must explain that date/time are preferences and require studio confirmation.

## 8. Accessibility Guardrails

- Test all text/background combinations for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Do not use Rose Pink as small text on Warm Ivory without verifying contrast.
- Use Deep Rose Brown for body text.
- Pair color changes with labels, borders, icons, or other visual cues.
- Provide visible keyboard focus and accessible names for controls.

## 9. Design Reference Strategy

Use Ashley & Co as a reference for editorial restraint, warm surfaces, typography hierarchy, and image-led storytelling. Maison Belle must retain its own Rose Pink identity rather than copying another brand's visual system.

The resulting system is an adaptation: editorial structure + rose-pink beauty identity + clear conversion-focused booking UX.
