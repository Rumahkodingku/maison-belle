# Maison Belle — Design Brief

**Project:** Maison Belle  
**Owner / Showcase publisher:** RumahKodingku  
**Project type:** Conceptual beauty studio website showcase  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved direction for design and product planning

---

## 1. Project Overview

Maison Belle is a fictional premium beauty studio created as a RumahKodingku showcase project. The website demonstrates how a thoughtfully designed digital experience can help a beauty business present its services, build trust, and convert visitors into appointment inquiries.

The business concept combines four service families:

- Hair & Beauty
- Nails
- Makeup
- Lash & Brow

The website is a marketing and inquiry-generation experience. Appointment requests are composed on the website and handed off to the studio through WhatsApp Business. The website does not confirm appointments or guarantee schedule availability.

## 2. Business Objectives

1. Present the studio's services and treatment information clearly.
2. Build confidence through professional imagery, artist profiles, testimonials, and portfolio work.
3. Make pricing understandable through fixed prices and “mulai dari” prices.
4. Reduce friction between discovering a service and requesting an appointment.
5. Generate qualified appointment inquiries through WhatsApp Business.
6. Demonstrate RumahKodingku's capabilities in brand-led web design, frontend engineering, conversion-focused UX, SEO, and accessibility.

### Primary conversion

A visitor completes the guided booking flow and opens WhatsApp with a prefilled appointment request.

### Supporting conversions

- Viewing service details.
- Exploring the gallery.
- Opening the contact/location information.
- Following the studio's social channels.

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary audience

Women aged approximately 18–35 who are interested in personal care, beauty treatments, nail services, and makeup for everyday or special occasions.

### Audience segments

| Segment              | Need                                                 | Website content that helps                 |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Beauty maintenance   | Hair, nail, lash, or brow care                       | Service catalog, prices, duration          |
| Event-ready customer | Makeup for graduation, party, engagement, or wedding | Makeup portfolio, service details, booking |
| Nail enthusiast      | Nail art, gel polish, extensions                     | Nail gallery, treatment options            |
| First-time visitor   | Confidence and clarity before choosing a salon       | About, artist profiles, testimonials, FAQ  |

Do not assume all visitors have the same budget, beauty preferences, or level of familiarity with salon treatments. Use clear, welcoming language and explain specialist terms where useful.

## 4. Brand Positioning

**Positioning statement**

Maison Belle is a welcoming premium beauty studio that brings hair, nail, makeup, and lash & brow services together in one considered experience.

**Brand promise**

A thoughtful beauty experience, presented with care from discovery to appointment inquiry.

**Tagline**

“Cantik dengan caramu.”

Alternative campaign line: “Your Beauty, Your Confidence.”

### Brand personality

- Elegant, not intimidating.
- Feminine, not childish.
- Warm, not overly sweet.
- Professional, not clinical.
- Contemporary, not trend-dependent.
- Detail-oriented, not visually crowded.

### Brand values

1. Care — communicate services and processes thoughtfully.
2. Confidence — help customers make informed choices.
3. Craft — celebrate the skill of beauty professionals.
4. Clarity — make services, pricing, and booking expectations transparent.

## 5. Creative Direction: Editorial Luxury

The visual language takes inspiration from premium beauty editorials and contemporary lifestyle magazines. The experience should feel art-directed, tactile, spacious, and image-led.

### Visual principles

- Use expressive editorial typography with restrained serif headlines.
- Pair warm neutral surfaces with deep brown text and muted blush accents.
- Use high-quality, authentic-looking beauty photography.
- Build compositions with asymmetry, generous whitespace, and deliberate cropping.
- Keep navigation and booking actions easy to find.
- Use subtle transitions rather than decorative animation everywhere.
- Use borders and dividers sparingly.
- Keep mobile layouts intentional; do not simply shrink desktop compositions.

### Initial color direction

| Role       | Color         | Hex       |
| ---------- | ------------- | --------- |
| Background | Warm ivory    | `#F7F1EB` |
| Surface    | Soft white    | `#FFFCF8` |
| Primary    | Deep cocoa    | `#49362F` |
| Accent     | Muted blush   | `#D8B6A8` |
| Text       | Warm charcoal | `#594B44` |
| Muted text | Taupe         | `#897A72` |

These are starting tokens and should be checked for contrast before final release.

### Typography direction

- Display/headings: Cormorant Garamond (or a metrically appropriate serif alternative).
- Body/UI: DM Sans (or a metrically appropriate sans-serif alternative).
- Use serif for expression and sans-serif for usability.
- Avoid long all-caps paragraphs and excessively small body text.

## 6. Experience Principles

1. **Beauty first, clarity always:** visual storytelling must not hide service information.
2. **Book without uncertainty:** explain that selected dates and times are preferences pending studio confirmation.
3. **Price transparency:** distinguish fixed prices from starting prices.
4. **Proof before promise:** use real portfolio work and genuine testimonials when the brand becomes real. For the fictional showcase, label sample content appropriately.
5. **Mobile-first conversion:** make service discovery and booking comfortable on a phone.
6. **Respectful data handling:** collect only the information needed to prepare an appointment inquiry.

## 7. Information Architecture

Recommended routes:

- `/` — Home
- `/services` — Service catalog
- `/services/[slug]` — Service detail
- `/gallery` — Portfolio gallery
- `/about` — Studio and artists
- `/contact` — Location and contact
- `/booking` — Guided booking

For the MVP, some content may also appear as homepage sections. Keep route structure extensible without creating unnecessary pages.

### Homepage section order

1. Header / navigation
2. Hero
3. Service categories
4. Featured treatments
5. Brand story
6. Editorial gallery preview
7. Meet the artists
8. Pricing highlights
9. Testimonials
10. Booking call-to-action
11. Location, opening hours, and contact
12. FAQ
13. Footer

## 8. Content and Photography Direction

### Image categories

- Hero: expressive beauty portrait or carefully art-directed studio image.
- Hair: hair styling, treatment, and healthy-hair detail.
- Nails: manicure, nail art, gel polish, and close-up craftsmanship.
- Makeup: natural, party, and bridal/editorial makeup.
- Lash & Brow: close-up treatment imagery.
- Studio: welcoming interior and treatment environment.
- Artists: professional portraits and in-action images.

### Image rules

- Prefer original or properly licensed imagery.
- Do not imply fictional work is a real customer's result.
- Avoid misleading before/after claims.
- Use consistent warmth and color grading.
- Provide meaningful alt text for informative images.
- Crop with focal-point awareness, especially on mobile.

## 9. Tone of Voice

Use Bahasa Indonesia that is warm, confident, polished, and easy to understand.

Preferred:

- “Pilih perawatan yang sesuai dengan kebutuhanmu.”
- “Lihat pilihan layanan dan estimasi harga.”
- “Kirim permintaan jadwal melalui WhatsApp.”
- “Tim kami akan membantu mengonfirmasi ketersediaan.”

Avoid:

- Overclaiming results.
- Medical or guaranteed beauty claims.
- Excessive slang.
- Pressure tactics and false urgency.
- Saying “booking berhasil” before the studio confirms.

## 10. Conversion Strategy

Primary CTA: **Booking Sekarang**  
Secondary CTA: **Jelajahi Layanan**

Contextual CTAs:

- “Lihat Detail”
- “Pilih Layanan Ini”
- “Lihat Hasil Perawatan”
- “Tanyakan melalui WhatsApp”

The booking CTA should be visible in the header and repeated after meaningful decision points, without becoming intrusive.

## 11. Showcase Deliverables

- Responsive website.
- Service catalog and service detail views.
- Mixed editorial gallery with category filtering and lightbox.
- Guided booking wizard.
- WhatsApp Click to Chat handoff.
- SEO metadata and structured data.
- Accessibility and performance checks.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile screenshots.
- Case study explaining the fictional business problem and the implemented solution.

## 12. Out of Scope for MVP

- Admin dashboard.
- Backend booking database.
- Real-time availability or slot locking.
- Customer accounts.
- Online payments or deposits.
- Automated reminders.
- Staff scheduling.
- Inventory and point-of-sale.
- Review submission system.
- WhatsApp API automation or webhook processing.

## 13. Definition of Design Success

The design is successful when a first-time visitor can quickly understand what Maison Belle offers, evaluate relevant services and indicative prices, trust the studio through its presentation, and submit an appointment request without confusion.
